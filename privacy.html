Who this is for

Hermes EA is a personal assistant service run by Peter Harrison for his own use. It is not offered to the public. The only user is Peter Harrison.

What is collected

  • The user's mobile phone number.
  • The text messages sent to and from the service.
  • Calendar entries and reminders that the user connects to the service.

How it is used

Only to send the user reminders and schedule updates, to receive and understand the user's replies, and to send replies. Nothing is used for advertising or marketing.

Service providers

Text messages are delivered through Twilio. The text of messages and calendar details are processed by the AI model provider that the assistant uses to write and read messages. Message content is generated by an AI assistant. These providers handle data only to run the service.

No sharing for marketing

Mobile information, including phone numbers and text messaging opt-in consent, is not shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Retention

Conversation history on the service, including text messages, is kept for 90 days and then deleted. Twilio, which delivers the messages, keeps its own delivery records under its own retention policy. Calendar data is read when needed and is not published.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to stop all messages.
  • Reply HELP for help.
  • To ask for deletion of stored messages, email pah0315@gmail.com.
