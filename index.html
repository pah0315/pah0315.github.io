Design Principles

Every system I build follows the same architecture:

Works immediately

Something useful happens before you understand the internals.

Transparent

You can see what it's doing and why. No black boxes.

You own the output

Files you control. Artifacts you can edit. Nothing locked behind my access.

Progressive depth

Use it simply. Go deeper when you're ready. Complexity is available, not imposed.

How I Work

The throughline: do the work, notice the pattern, encode the pattern, package it for adoption, teach the methodology. Learn. Build. Teach. Repeat.

I don't build tools from the outside looking in. I work the queue daily, hit the same problems repeatedly, and turn those problems into systems. The investigation methodology I use 15+ times a day is the same methodology encoded into the AI skills. That's why the automation works: it's grounded in real operational experience.

On AI: I use Cursor as my primary development environment. Every system on this page was built there. I treat AI as a force multiplier with verification requirements, not a replacement for judgment. The prompts matter less than the habits around them.
